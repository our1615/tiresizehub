Privacy Policy

At Tire Size Hub, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

No Personal Data Collection

Our calculators and tools run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or calculation inputs. All data stays on your device.

Cookies

We may use essential cookies to ensure the proper functioning of the website. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that identify individual users.

Advertising

We may display advertisements through third-party networks. These networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
