🔋 Recommended EV Tires

🔧 How It Works

Electric vehicles are 500–1,000 lbs heavier than their gas counterparts and produce instant torque that scrubs rubber aggressively. EV-specific tires use reinforced sidewalls (higher load indexes), acoustic foam layers to reduce cabin noise, and low-rolling-resistance compounds to maximize range. This matcher looks up your vehicle in our database and returns factory specs plus recommended EV tires that meet or exceed those specs.

How to use: Select your EV make, model, and year. Click Find EV Tires to see factory size, minimum load index, speed rating, and vehicle weight. Browse the recommended tires, each tagged with features like acoustic foam and reinforced sidewalls.

Tip: Never downgrade the load index on an EV. The extra weight and torque will destroy tires rated for lighter vehicles. Look for tires with "HL" (High Load) or "XL" (Extra Load) markings if available for your size.
